Tape Run Assessment and Compliance Engine

Acceptance Criteria
Power box load
Total load must stay under 96 W.
Tape per controller
Stay under the controller limit for the selected tape style.
Tape length
One-end feed: 16.4 ft max. Both-end feed: modeled as two half-length feeds.
Start of Tape
Light loss before the tape starts, based on wire length, wire size, and total load.
Start to End of Tape
Brightness difference from the tape start to the far end.
Result ranges
Under 25% is good. 25-40% may be visible. Over 40% is not recommended.
